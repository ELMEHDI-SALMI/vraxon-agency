Why Most E-Commerce Stores Lose 97% of Their Visitors

Driving traffic is expensive. If your store suffers from these 4 silent revenue killers, scaling paid ads only wastes your margin.

High Cart Abandonment

Complicated checkout flows, unexpected fees, and missing trust badges cause 70%+ of shoppers to leave right before paying.

Mobile Conversion Friction

Slow load speeds, clumsy mobile navigation, and small touch targets erode conversions on smartphones where 80% of traffic lands.

Weak Value Proposition

Generic product copy and unclear key benefits fail to build immediate value, leading to high bounce rates within 5 seconds.

Guesswork Decision Making

Redesigning based on intuition instead of analytics, heatmaps, and structured A/B testing usually breaks existing sales flows.
